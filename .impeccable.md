## Design Context

### Users
Personal daily tracking app used by the owner and a small circle of close friends. Used throughout the day — morning goal-setting, midday check-ins, evening reflection. The user is the primary audience; close friends are secondary. Context is intimate, not public. The app is always-on, always nearby.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Calm. Clean. Medical.

The app should feel like a trusted health companion — precise without being cold, personal without being cluttered. Think: a beautifully designed clinical journal, or a premium wellness app. Not a productivity dashboard, not a social feed. Quiet confidence.

Emotional goals: **welcoming on open, easy to use, calming in use, exciting to engage with**. The user wants to *want* to open it — not because it's gamified or loud, but because it feels good to be in.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Light mode first**: Medical and clinical UIs are light. The current dark-first approach feels intense; shift to a clean off-white/warm-neutral light mode as the primary experience. Dark mode should remain as an option but be the secondary theme.
- **Refined minimalism**: Lots of breathing room. Not everything needs a card. Space is the primary design material.
- **Softer palette**: Move away from electric violet (AI slop) toward a calm, desaturated teal-blue as the primary accent. Pillar colors (work/future/body) stay but become more muted and pastel-adjacent — less neon, more clinical reference colors.
- **Better typography**: Inter is invisible and generic. Pair a humanist sans-serif body font (DM Sans — slightly warmer, more personal) with a subtle serif or display weight for headings (Instrument Serif — adds warmth without being decorative). This creates the "calm but exciting" duality.
- **Reduce glassmorphism**: The heavy `backdrop-filter: blur()` on sticky headers and nav bars feels intense and unfocused. Replace with clean opaque surfaces with subtle borders.
- **Purposeful animation**: Slower, breath-like transitions. Remove the `springIn` bounce. Entrances should feel like something gently settling, not bouncing onto screen.

**Anti-references**: Avoid anything that looks like: a productivity/hustle dashboard, a crypto/fintech app, a dark hacker terminal, a generic "AI app" with purple gradients and glow effects.

**References**: Apple Health, Oura Ring app, Readwise, Linear (calm precision), Bear app (warmth + clarity).

### Design Principles
1. **Breathing room over density** — Generous whitespace signals that nothing is urgent. The app should feel like a pause, not a push.
2. **Meaningful color, never decorative** — Each color earns its place (work=blue, future=amber, body=green, accent=teal). No gradients for decoration, no glow effects.
3. **Typography carries the hierarchy** — Size + weight + spacing should make structure obvious. Color is secondary.
4. **Calm in motion** — Animations should be gentle and purposeful. Ease-out, never bounce. Motion should feel like breathing, not bouncing.
5. **Personal, not clinical coldness** — Medical precision in layout, but warmth in color temperature, font choice, and copy tone. Clean, not sterile.
